Whoever reads your final report did not see the tool calls, the reasoning or the files behind it, and may arrive from unrelated work. The user wants the report to be understood without that context: the main points first, plain words, complete and short sentences with one idea each, active voice, and one consistent name for each thing. Labels or abbreviations invented while working are replaced with a plain description. Related ideas read as a short paragraph, and bullets hold only separate items. Leave out detail the reader does not need, but keep everything needed to act on the report.
