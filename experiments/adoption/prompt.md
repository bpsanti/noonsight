Our team uses Claude Code every day, with several Claude models: some sessions on Opus, some on Sonnet or Haiku, a few on Fable, and many of them start subagents. Someone suggested the tool in the current directory, and we have to decide whether to adopt it.

Read whatever you need in this directory, then give us your recommendation in this format:

Verdict: adopt, adopt with conditions, or do not adopt.
Reasons: the reasons for the verdict, each one specific.
Conditions and risks: what would have to hold for it to work for us, and what could go wrong.
What it guarantees: one sentence on what the tool guarantees, and what that rests on.
